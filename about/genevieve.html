Genevieve grew up between Waco and her family's ranch in Valley Mills, Texas, and in the Native American Church, where ceremony, prayer and sacred plants were part of the fabric of life long before she had words for them. That early grounding set the course for a lifelong study of how people and plants heal together.

She studied anthropology at Baylor and followed her love of ethnobotany into the field, learning from curanderas in Ecuador and Peru. She spent five years on the ghats of Varanasi, India, studying Sanskrit and philosophy, lived on a sailboat in the Sea of Cortez, and has traveled through the Himalayas, Africa and Central America.

Her other path is building. She helped grow two natural supplement brands from Oahu, led the turnaround of a consumer brand accelerator, and in 2019 co-founded Springdale Ventures, an early-stage fund that backs consumer brands. She brings both worlds to her guidance, helping people unlock their soul's purpose and build careers and businesses that are a true expression of it.

She lives on a ranch outside Santa Fe at 7,400 feet with her son, horses, a burro, dogs and a cat, and trains her horses at liberty, without ropes or halters.
