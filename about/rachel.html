Rachel is a spiritual guide who listens for the patterns beneath the surface of a life: the early stories that shaped us, the strengths we have not yet claimed, and the wisdom the body is already holding. Her guidance is rooted in depth and Jungian psychology and in the transpersonal traditions, where dreams, symbols and the natural world are all teachers.

A graduate of Southwestern College in Santa Fe, she weaves somatic awareness, imagery, dreamwork, the Enneagram and astrology into her sessions, and leads ceremony and nature immersion on the land. Her presence is relational and collaborative, grounded in care, curiosity and a deep attunement to the nervous system.

She is especially drawn to sensitive, creative and neurodivergent souls, to people on nonlinear paths, and to anyone seeking meaning in a season of grief, overwhelm or transition. At the heart of her work is a simple belief: the light you are looking for is already within you. Her role is to help you remember it.
