What it is

Nine patterns of the personality, and the path of growth hidden inside each one.

The Enneagram maps nine core types, each organized around an unconscious motivation: a deep longing and a matching fear that shaped how you learned to move through the world. These patterns were brilliant survival strategies once. Many of them are simply too heavy to carry now.

Each type expresses itself through one of three instincts: Self-Preservation, Social or One-to-One. Together they make 27 subtypes, which is why two people of the same type can look so different.
