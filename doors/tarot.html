What it is

Archetypal images that open a conversation with your intuition.

A tarot deck holds 78 cards. The 22 cards of the Major Arcana trace the great journey of the soul, from the Fool stepping off the cliff to the World made whole. The 56 cards of the Minor Arcana, in four suits, speak to the everyday textures of feeling, thought, action and the material world.

In a reading, the images become a mirror. They give shape to what you already sense but have not yet said, and they open a conversation with the part of you that knows.

What it illuminates
