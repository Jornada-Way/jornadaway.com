What it is

Ritual on the land. The oldest teacher, and often the most honest one.

Every tradition of awakening began outdoors. Ceremony is how we slow down enough to listen: to the seasons, to the elements, to each other and to the quiet voice beneath our own noise.

Our ceremonial work is grounded in respect for the traditions it comes from and for the land that holds it. It ranges from a simple walk with intention to a full weekend of ritual and immersion in nature.
