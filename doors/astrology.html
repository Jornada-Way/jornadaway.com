What it is

The map of the sky at the moment you arrived, read as a story about who you are becoming.

Your birth chart is a snapshot of the heavens at the exact time and place you entered the world. The planets describe the parts of you, the signs describe their style, and the houses describe where in life they come to play.

We read astrology as a living language rather than a fixed fate. Your Sun, Moon and Rising sign are only the opening lines. The aspects between planets and the transits moving through your chart tell the story of what is ripening in you right now.
